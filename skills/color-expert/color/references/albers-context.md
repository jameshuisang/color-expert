# albers-context — contextual observation

Reviewed 2026-11-14. Selected passages only. Cross-media recommendations are project synthesis.
https://www.albersfoundation.org/alberses/teaching/interaction-of-color
The foundation describes Albers's experimental teaching and quotes his 1963 book: colors must be studied in relation to their surroundings. Read the teaching discussion, particularly its contextual exercises. This supports comparing applied arrangements instead of trusting isolated samples. It does not prove every proposed combination will evoke the same response in everyone.
