# golden-mixing — physical paint behavior

Reviewed 2026-11-14. Selected passages only. Cross-media recommendations are project synthesis.
https://goldenartistcolors.com/inspiration/digital-color-wheel
Read the manufacturer's publicly accessible wheel-making instructions. They distinguish masstone, undertone, tinting and water dilution, and explain that a visual midpoint may not be a 1:1 mixture because tinting strength varies. This supports making physical mixing studies instead of substituting digital interpolation. It does not establish accurate results for arbitrary pigments, brands or supports.
