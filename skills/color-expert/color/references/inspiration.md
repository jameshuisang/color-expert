# Inspiration and provenance

Record source author/product, screen, mode, version/date, URL, rights and whether values are sampled or official. A screenshot is an observation under specific rendering conditions, not proof of official tokens. Ask for authorised references when relevant; do not assume a particular brand palette.

Keep imported private assets outside the distributable. Preserve attribution and distinguish inspiration from standards, measured evidence and user preferences. This candidate includes no captured product palettes or screenshots.
