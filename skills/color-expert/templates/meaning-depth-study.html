Meaning needs context.
Quiet need not mean flat.

Two controlled teaching contrasts. These are design hypotheses to inspect, not claims that a color causes an emotion or proves audience preference.

1. One red, two roles

A note of affection

A warm invitation to someone you care about.

A destructive action

Remove this project permanently.

Both accents are exactly #B82F42. The labels, icons and situation differ. These are inert illustration buttons; nothing is sent or deleted. Association is not a guarantee of felt emotion or behaviour.

2. Same geometry, different plane relationships

A · Compressed relationships

Similar pale values across roof, interior and distance can compress depth. A deliberately flat graphic may want this effect.

B · Differentiated relationships

A darker interior and foreground frame the lighter seating and roof. Cooler supporting planes provide another separation cue.

This contrast changes several color relationships together; it is not a single-variable causal experiment. Next isolate value, hue and chroma changes separately. Stronger depth is not automatically better beauty or better fidelity.
