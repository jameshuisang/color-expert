# Pair judgements: compact routing note

Schloss & Palmer (2011), [Aesthetic response to color combinations](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13414-010-0027-0). Main methods/results/discussion and appendix reviewed 2026-11-16; relevant supplement checked. No raw-data reanalysis.

Distinguish whole-pair harmony, whole-pair liking and liking a named foreground on its ground. The study favoured similar hues for whole pairs, but foreground hue-contrast benefits depended on lightness conditions. Liking also involved component preferences and lightness contrast; harmony is neither liking nor mere similarity. Group trends do not determine an individual's taste or a story's success.
