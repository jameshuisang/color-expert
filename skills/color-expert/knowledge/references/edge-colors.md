# Edge-color inspiration and ordered ramps
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qiXHiABcl-I
Color Nerd, *Goethe's “Edge Colors” are Actually a Design Hack*. Reviewed 2026-11-14; complete available transcript 0:00–11:12 / 11:16. No visual review.

**0:34–3:12:** Uses prism edge-color sequences to discuss coupled hue/lightness paths. Treat the optical diagram and complementary-light claims as creator explanation, not a verified spectral simulation.
**3:21–4:40:** Advocates ordered lightness, discrimination and perceptual step spacing for readable ramps. These are separate checks; one does not establish the others.
**5:02–6:31:** Discusses viridis/magma trajectories. Do not infer their actual endpoints from the spoken black-to-white description.
**6:40–11:12:** Demonstrates parameterized hue, saturation and brightness curves. Tool settings are example choices, not timeless constraints. Curved parameter functions are not mathematically linear merely because the palette is ordered.

Operational consequence: decide data semantics before choosing a ramp; inspect ordering and steps. A pleasing illustration gradient is not automatically a good quantitative map or accessible text background. The new ramp checker tests sample luminance order only, not perceptual uniformity or color-vision accessibility.
